Particle Pop 조사 팡

Particles stick to the end of a word and say what job it does in the sentence. Pop the particle that fills the gap.

Cheat sheet
  • 은 / 는: topic, “as for…”: introductions, contrast. 저는 학생이에요.
  • 이 / 가: subject, with 있다/없다, 아니다, how something is, new information. 고양이가 있어요.
  • 을 / 를: object of an action. 사과를 먹어요.
  • The first form follows a consonant (책은, 책이, 책을), the second a vowel (사과는, 사과가, 사과를).
  • 에: to / at (places you go, where things are, times). 에서: where an action happens. 도: also.
