Syllable Builder 음절 만들기

Every Korean syllable is a block: a first consonant, a vowel, and sometimes a final consonant (받침) underneath. Hear a syllable, then build it from its letters. Tall vowels (ㅏ ㅓ ㅣ) sit to the right, flat ones (ㅗ ㅜ ㅡ) underneath.

Keys: Enter checks / continues, Backspace removes a letter, Space replays.
